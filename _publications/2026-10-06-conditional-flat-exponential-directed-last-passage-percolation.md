We study line-to-point exponential directed last passage percolation with a line boundary, conditioned on an atypically large last-passage time to a fixed endpoint. We develop heuristic predictions for the conditional limit shape and prove fluctuation results in certain regimes using steepest-descent analysis of exact multi-point formulas. Our calculations suggest that conditional fluctuations near the diagonal are described by Brownian bridges coupled through a shared Gaussian component.
