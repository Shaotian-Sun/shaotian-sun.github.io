## Research Interests

My current research interests include:

- Integrable probability: Passage percolations, Interacting particle systems
- High-dimensional latent variable models: Ranking models (Intransitive comparsion model)
- Recommendation Systems

## Current Research

### Conitional Exponential Directed Last-Passage Percolation

I am working with **Professor Jinho Baik** on directed last-passage percolation with Rishi. Our current work focuses on fluctuation behavior with step and flat initial conditions, and extensions to more general boundary geometries conditional on an upper large deviation event.

### High-Dimensional Statistical Inference

Under the guidance of **Professor Gongjun Xu**, I am studying identifiability, both average and uniform consistency, and asymptotic inference for Blade-Chest-Inner Model, an inransitive pairwise comparison model.

## Current Project

### KuaiFlow: Two-Tower Recommendation Retrieval

I am building an end-to-end recommendation retrieval system on the KuaiRand short-video interaction dataset, with a focus on scalable candidate generation and realistic offline evaluation.

**Tech**: Python, PyTorch, FAISS, NumPy, Pandas, recommendation systems, approximate nearest-neighbor search
